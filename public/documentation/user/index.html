Nail Salon App — User Guide

Welcome! This guide will walk you through everything you need to know to run your day-to-day work in the Nail Salon App. No technical knowledge required.

Introduction

What is the Nail Salon App?

The Nail Salon App is an all-in-one system for managing your nail salon. It helps you handle customers, appointments, technicians, payments, and reports — all in one place.

What problem does it solve?

Instead of using paper booking sheets, separate calculators for tips and commissions, and different tools for customer records, everything you need is bundled into one easy-to-use app. You can:

  • Keep track of walk-in and booked customers
  • Assign technicians fairly based on turn order
  • Process payments, tips, and discounts quickly
  • Keep a full history of every customer visit
  • See how much each technician has earned

Who is it for?

The app is designed for three types of users:

  • Admin The salon owner or manager — has full access to everything.
  • Receptionist Front-desk staff who greet customers, book appointments, and process payments.
  • Technician Nail technicians who clock in/out and see their own appointments and earnings.

Getting Started

How to access the app

Open your web browser (Chrome, Edge, Firefox, or Safari) and go to the address given to you by your manager — for example:

Example

https://yoursalon.com

Logging in

  1. Go to the login page.
  2. Enter your email (or username) and password.
  3. Click the Login button.
  4. You'll be taken to your dashboard.
Who creates my account? Accounts are created by the salon admin. If you don't have one yet, please ask your manager.

Forgot your password?

  1. On the login page, click Forgot Password.
  2. Enter the email address linked to your account.
  3. Follow the instructions on the screen to set a new password.

First-time setup (for Admins)

If you're setting up the app for the first time as an admin, we recommend doing these steps in order:

  1. Go to Settings and fill in your salon name, currency, tax rate, and business hours.
  2. Add your Services (for example: Gel Manicure, Pedicure, Nail Art) with prices.
  3. Add your Technicians and staff under User Management.
  4. Set up Commission rates in Settings.
  5. Optional: connect to GoHighLevel (GHL) if you use it for bookings.

Dashboard Overview

The Dashboard is the first screen you see after logging in. It gives you a quick snapshot of the day.

What you'll see

  • Today's numbers: total customers, technicians on duty, and the number of tickets waiting, in progress, or unpaid.
  • Latest Tickets: the most recent tickets created — you can jump to any of them with one click.
  • Quick actions: shortcuts to create an appointment, open the waiting list, or go to the calendar.

Technicians see a simpler view

If you're a technician, your dashboard focuses on your day: your assigned customers, the services you've completed, your tips and commissions, plus a big Clock In / Clock Out button.

Tip: Use the sidebar menu on the left to move between sections at any time.

Waiting List

What it does

The Waiting List is where you add walk-in customers who have just arrived, and where booked customers appear when they arrive for their appointment.

Adding a walk-in customer

  1. Open the Waiting List from the sidebar.
  2. Click + Add Customer.
  3. Search for the customer by name or phone. If they're new, click Create new customer and enter their name and phone number.
  4. Pick the services they want.
  5. (Optional) Assign a technician now, or leave it unassigned and decide later.
  6. Click Save. The customer now appears in the waiting list.

Filtering and searching

  • Use the tabs at the top to filter by Walk-in or Booked.
  • Use the search box to find a customer by name.
Example Use Case

A customer walks in at 2:00 PM asking for a pedicure. You add them to the Waiting List with the "Pedicure" service. A minute later, Technician Anna becomes available, so you assign Anna to the customer and move them to Tickets.

Calendar & Bookings

What it does

The Calendar shows all booked appointments for the day, week, or month. You can create new bookings, move them to a new time, or cancel them.

Switching views

At the top of the Calendar you can pick between:

  • Grid view — a traditional calendar layout.
  • Technician view — one column per technician, which makes it easy to see who is busy when.

Creating a booking

  1. Click an empty time slot on the calendar.
  2. Select or create a customer.
  3. Pick the services and date/time.
  4. (Optional) Assign a technician.
  5. Click Save.

Rescheduling an appointment

Simply click and drag an appointment to its new time slot. The change is saved automatically.

Understanding the colors

  • Each appointment is color-coded by its status (waiting, in-progress, unpaid, paid, cancelled).
  • Appointments with no technician yet appear in a "Not Assigned" column.
Tip: Click any appointment to see its full details — customer info, services, technician, and payment status.

Tickets

What is a Ticket?

A Ticket is created when a customer's service is finished and they're ready to pay. Think of it as the bill or receipt in progress.

Ticket statuses

StatusMeaning
UnpaidService is done, waiting for the customer to pay.
PaidPayment has been collected.
CancelledThe service was cancelled before finishing.
RefundedPayment was taken, then returned to the customer.

Viewing tickets

Go to Tickets from the sidebar. Use the tabs at the top to switch between Unpaid, Paid, Cancelled, and Refunded.

Editing a ticket

  1. Click the ticket you want to change.
  2. Add or remove services, update quantities, or change the technician.
  3. Click Save.
Heads up: Once a ticket is marked Paid, changes are limited. For mistakes after payment, use the Refund option.

Payments

Taking a payment

  1. Open the unpaid ticket.
  2. Click Pay or Checkout.
  3. Review the services, quantities, and totals.
  4. Add any of the following as needed:
    • Discount — apply a coupon code.
    • Customer credits — use loyalty credits the customer has saved up.
    • Gift card — enter the gift card PIN to deduct the balance.
    • Tip — enter the amount the customer is tipping.
  5. Confirm the final total.
  6. Click Complete Payment.

How tips are split

If more than one technician worked on the appointment, tips are split evenly between them by default. You can adjust the split per technician on the payment screen.

Refunds

  1. Open the paid ticket.
  2. Click Refund.
  3. Enter a short note explaining the reason.
  4. Confirm. The ticket moves to the Refunded tab.
Example Use Case

A customer gets a $40 manicure and tips $8. Two technicians shared the work. The system automatically splits the tip as $4 each, and calculates commission on the service amount for each technician.

Customers

What you can do

  • Keep a list of every customer who has ever visited.
  • Store phone numbers, emails, and photos.
  • See a customer's full visit history and total spend.
  • Track loyalty credits earned.

Adding a new customer

  1. Go to Customers in the sidebar.
  2. Click + Add Customer.
  3. Fill in their name, phone, and any other details you want.
  4. Click Save.

Viewing a customer

Click any customer in the list to see their profile — past visits, services they usually get, credit balance, and contact info.

Tip: Use the search bar at the top of the Customers page to find someone quickly by name or phone number.

Technicians

What you'll find here

The Technicians page lists everyone who performs services at the salon. For each one you can see:

  • Commission earned (for a selected date range)
  • Total tips
  • Number of services completed
  • Clock-in / clock-out history

Clocking in / out Technician

  1. Go to your Dashboard.
  2. Click the Clock In button when you start your shift.
  3. At the end of your shift, click Clock Out.
Why it matters: Clocking in tells the system you're available so the receptionist can assign customers to you through the Turn Tracker.

Turn Tracker

What it does

The Turn Tracker helps distribute walk-in customers fairly among technicians. It shows who is clocked in right now and how many services each has already done today.

How assignment works

Based on your Settings, the app can suggest the next technician either by:

  • Lowest-first: the technician with the fewest services today gets the next customer (most common — keeps the workload even).
  • Highest-first: the opposite, used in some salons that prioritize seniority.
Example Use Case

Three technicians clock in at 10:00 AM. By noon, Anna has done 3 services, Ben has done 1, and Cara has done 2. The Turn Tracker suggests Ben for the next walk-in, because he has the lowest count.

Services

Managing your service menu Admin

The Services page is where you list everything your salon offers: Manicure, Pedicure, Gel, Nail Art, and so on.

Adding a service

  1. Go to Services.
  2. Click + Add Service.
  3. Enter a name, price, description, and pick a category (e.g. "Manicure", "Pedicure").
  4. Upload a photo if you'd like.
  5. Pick a color so it stands out on the calendar.
  6. Click Save.

Hiding a service temporarily

You don't have to delete a service you're not offering right now. Simply toggle it to Inactive — it will no longer appear when creating a new appointment, but its history is preserved.

Coupons & Gift Cards

Coupons

Coupons are discount codes you can apply at checkout.

  1. Go to Coupons in the sidebar.
  2. Click + Add Coupon.
  3. Choose the discount type: Percentage (e.g. 10% off) or Fixed amount (e.g. $5 off).
  4. (Optional) Set a minimum order amount.
  5. Save. The coupon code is now ready to use during payment.

Gift Cards

Gift cards work like prepaid balances — a customer buys one, gets a PIN, and can spend it on services later.

  1. Go to Gift Cards.
  2. Click + New Gift Card.
  3. Enter the starting amount (e.g. $50).
  4. Give the auto-generated PIN to the customer.
  5. When the customer uses it at checkout, enter the PIN on the payment screen and the balance is deducted automatically.
Tip: A gift card can be used across multiple visits until its balance reaches zero.

Customer Credits (Loyalty)

What they are

Customer credits are loyalty rewards. Customers earn credits automatically when they pay, and can use those credits to pay for future services.

How credits are earned

The way credits are earned is set in Settings. You can pick one of two rules:

  • Fixed amount per visit — e.g. 5 credits every time a customer pays.
  • Percentage of the bill — e.g. 10% of the subtotal becomes credits.

Using credits at checkout

On the payment screen, type in how many credits to apply and the app deducts them from the customer's balance and from the total due.

Adjusting manually Admin

If you need to give bonus credits (e.g. a "sorry for the wait" gesture), open the customer's profile and add credits directly.

Payout Reports

What it's for Admin

The Payout Report shows how much each technician has earned in commissions and tips — exactly what you need for payday.

Running a payout report

  1. Go to Payout in the sidebar.
  2. Pick a date range (for example "Last week" or pick custom dates).
  3. The page shows every technician and their totals.
  4. You can print or export the report to share with your accountant.

Account Settings

Updating your profile

  1. Click your name or profile picture in the top-right corner.
  2. Choose Profile (or My Account).
  3. Change your name, email, or profile photo.
  4. Click Save.

Changing your password

  1. Open your profile page.
  2. Click Change Password.
  3. Enter your current password, then your new password twice.
  4. Click Save.
Keep your password private: don't share your login with coworkers. Every user should have their own account.

Logging out

Click your name in the top-right corner and choose Logout. Always log out if you're using a shared computer.

Notifications & Alerts

The app shows small pop-up messages (called "toasts") at the top or bottom of the screen when something happens, for example:

  • Green — success (your change was saved).
  • Yellow — a warning you should notice.
  • Red — an error that needs your attention.
Example Use Case: After you finish a payment, a green message saying "Payment completed" appears. If the internet drops out mid-save, you'll see a red error and the app will ask you to try again.

Common Tasks

"A customer just walked in"

  1. Open the Waiting List.
  2. Click + Add Customer.
  3. Search or create the customer and pick their services.
  4. Assign a technician (or leave unassigned for now).

"The service is done, they want to pay"

  1. Find the customer's ticket under the Unpaid tab.
  2. Click Pay.
  3. Add any discount, tip, or gift card.
  4. Complete the payment.

"Customer wants to book for next week"

  1. Open the Calendar.
  2. Click the time slot on the date they want.
  3. Add the customer and services.
  4. Save.

"I need to move an appointment"

  1. Open the Calendar.
  2. Click and drag the appointment to the new time.
  3. Let go — it saves automatically.

"I clocked in at the wrong time"

  1. Ask an admin to adjust your clock-in on the Technicians page.
  2. Admins can fix entries under the technician's profile.

"Customer wants to add another service midway"

  1. Open their ticket from the Tickets page (or from the Waiting List if still waiting).
  2. Click Edit and add the extra service.
  3. Adjust the technician if someone else is doing the new service.
  4. Save — the new total will show automatically.

"Customer is a no-show"

  1. Open the Calendar or Waiting List and find the booking.
  2. Click the appointment and choose Mark as No-Show.
  3. The appointment is kept in the customer's history so you can track repeat no-shows.

"Customer wants to pay partly with a gift card and partly with cash"

  1. On the payment screen, enter the gift card PIN first — the balance is deducted.
  2. The remaining amount shows as the cash (or other) total to collect.
  3. Complete the payment as usual.

"Customer wants to use their saved loyalty credits"

  1. Open their ticket and click Pay.
  2. In the Credits field, type the amount of credits to apply.
  3. The total due drops by the same amount.
  4. Complete the payment.

"Split tip between two technicians"

  1. On the payment screen, enter the total tip amount.
  2. Below, adjust each technician's share so the amounts add up to the total.
  3. Complete the payment.

"Customer wants a receipt"

  1. Open the paid ticket from the Tickets page.
  2. Click Print Receipt (or View Receipt).
  3. You can print it for them or share the view on screen.

"Undo a wrong payment"

  1. Open the paid ticket.
  2. Click Refund.
  3. Add a short note (e.g. "Wrong amount, reprocessing").
  4. Create a new payment with the correct details.

"Add a new staff member"

Admin

  1. Go to User Management.
  2. Click + Add User.
  3. Enter their name, email, and pick a role (Admin, Receptionist, or Technician).
  4. Set a temporary password — ask them to change it on first login.
  5. Save. They can now log in.

"Issue a new gift card"

  1. Go to Gift Cards.
  2. Click + New Gift Card.
  3. Enter the starting amount.
  4. Hand the customer the generated PIN (or print the card).

"Add a walk-in with no service yet"

  1. Open the Waiting List and add the customer without selecting a service.
  2. When they decide, edit the entry and add the service.
  3. Then move to Tickets as usual.

"See today's earnings for a technician"

  1. Go to Technicians and click the name.
  2. Set the date range to "Today".
  3. Their commissions, tips, and service count are shown at the top.

"End of day — close out the shift"

  1. Make sure every Unpaid ticket is either paid or cancelled.
  2. Technicians click Clock Out from their dashboard.
  3. Admin can run a Payout Report to see the day's totals.

Troubleshooting

"I can't log in"

  • Double-check your email and password. Passwords are case-sensitive.
  • Make sure Caps Lock is off.
  • Click Forgot Password to reset it.
  • If that doesn't work, ask your admin to verify your account is active.

"The page won't load"

  • Check your internet connection.
  • Refresh the page (press F5 or the circular arrow in your browser).
  • If nothing changes, try again in a minute — the server might be restarting.

"I get an error when I save"

  • Make sure all required fields are filled in. Required fields are marked with a red asterisk (*).
  • Check the error message — it will usually tell you which field is wrong.
  • If it keeps happening, contact support (see below).

"The wrong technician got the tip"

  • Open the ticket and click Edit.
  • Adjust the technician list or the tip split.
  • Save. If the ticket is already paid, an admin may need to refund and reissue.

"I don't see a menu item my coworker has"

Some features are only visible to certain roles. For example, Payout Reports and User Management are Admin-only. If you need access, ask your manager.

"The Calendar is blank or missing appointments"

  • Make sure the correct date range is selected at the top of the page.
  • Clear any filters (technician, status) that might be hiding appointments.
  • Refresh the page (F5). If still empty, switch between Grid view and Technician view.

"I clicked Save but nothing happened"

  • Scroll up — there may be a red error message at the top of the form.
  • Look for fields highlighted in red; those need to be fixed.
  • If everything looks correct, try once more. A slow connection can delay the save.

"A customer's credit balance looks wrong"

  • Open the customer's profile and check the Credit Ledger — it lists every credit added or used.
  • The ledger is a full audit trail, so the balance is always the sum of entries.
  • If you spot a mistake, an admin can add a manual adjustment with a note.

"A gift card PIN isn't working"

  • Make sure you're typing the PIN exactly as printed — watch for the number 0 vs letter O.
  • Check under Gift Cards that the card isn't already fully used (balance = 0).
  • Confirm the card is still marked Active.

"A coupon code says 'invalid'"

  • Double-check the spelling — codes are case-sensitive.
  • Check the minimum order amount on the coupon; the ticket total may be below it.
  • Make sure the coupon is still Active (not expired or disabled by an admin).

"My profile photo won't upload"

  • Photos must be in JPG or PNG format.
  • Keep the file size small — under 5 MB works best.
  • If the upload seems stuck, refresh the page and try again.

"Two people are editing the same ticket"

  • Whoever saves last will overwrite earlier changes.
  • To avoid this, only one person should edit a ticket at a time — usually the receptionist handling that customer.
  • If something got overwritten, check the customer's history; the previous payment or edit may still be there.

"The Turn Tracker is suggesting the wrong technician"

  • Make sure the technician you expected is actually clocked in.
  • Check Settings — the ordering rule may be set to "highest-first" instead of "lowest-first".
  • The counter resets daily, so early in the day it only reflects today's services.

"I'm logged out unexpectedly"

  • For security, the app signs you out after a period of inactivity.
  • Simply log back in — your unsaved work from the last form may be lost, but saved records are safe.
  • If it keeps happening every few minutes, ask your admin to check session settings.

"GoHighLevel bookings aren't syncing"

Admin

  • Go to Settings and confirm the GHL integration is connected.
  • Tokens refresh automatically every hour. If disconnected, reconnect from Settings.
  • Synced bookings will appear in the Calendar a few seconds after being created in GHL.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need to install anything?
No. The app runs in your web browser. Just open the website address and log in.
Can I use the app on my phone?
Yes. The app works on phones and tablets. For comfort, a tablet or laptop is recommended for front-desk staff.
Will I lose data if the internet goes out?
Anything you saved is already stored on the server. If the connection drops while you're typing, you may need to re-enter the last few changes.
Can two receptionists use it at the same time?
Yes. Multiple people can be logged in and working at once. Each person should log in with their own account.
How do I give a customer a discount?
Apply a Coupon code at checkout. If you don't have a coupon code yet, an admin can create one under the Coupons page.
A customer wants a refund. What should I do?
Open the paid ticket and click Refund. Add a short note so there's a record of why.
Can I delete a customer from the system?
Admins can, but we don't recommend it — deleting a customer also removes their visit history. It's usually better to mark them inactive.
How does the commission get calculated?
Commission is based on the commission rate set in Settings, applied to the services each technician performed. The app does this automatically when you complete a payment.
Can I use the app offline?
No. The app needs an internet connection so your data stays up to date for everyone working at the same time.
What if a customer has two people on the same bill?
Each person should have their own ticket so services, tips, and payments are tracked correctly. You can ring up both tickets together at checkout.
Can I assign more than one technician to a single appointment?
Yes. When creating or editing the appointment, add all technicians who will be working on the service. Tips and commission can be split between them at payment time.
What happens if I forget to clock out?
An admin can correct your clock-out time from your technician profile. Try to clock out before you leave so your hours stay accurate.
How do I print the day's report?
Open the Payout page, pick "Today" as the date range, then press Ctrl + P (Windows) or Cmd + P (Mac) to print. You can also print from the Tickets page if you want the detailed list.
Can a technician see other technicians' earnings?
No. Technicians only see their own commissions and tips. The full payout report is Admin-only.
How do I change the tax rate or currency?
Go to Settings (Admin only). Tax rate, currency, and business info are at the top of that page.
Can I change a ticket's technician after payment?
Not directly — once a ticket is paid, the commission is locked in. If it's truly wrong, refund the ticket and re-create it with the correct technician.
What happens if a coupon and a gift card are used together?
The coupon discount is applied first, then the gift card balance is used against the reduced total. Any remainder is collected normally.
Can I send appointment reminders to customers?
If the salon uses GoHighLevel, reminders can be set up there and synced to the app. Otherwise, the app doesn't send reminders directly — your admin can confirm your salon's setup.
How do I search for a customer quickly?
Use the search box at the top of the Customers page. You can search by name, phone number, or email.
What if the customer's name already exists?
Pick the existing profile instead of creating a new one — that keeps their visit history and credits in one place.
How do I mark a service unavailable for today?
Go to Services, open the service, and toggle it to Inactive. Turn it back on when you're ready.
Why don't I see the Payout or Settings page?
Those are Admin-only sections. Receptionists and technicians have a focused menu that hides management tools they don't need.
How long is my data kept?
Visit history, payments, and ledger entries are kept permanently unless an admin deletes them. This helps you pull up a customer's history even years later.
Is my password safe?
Yes. Passwords are stored securely (encrypted) and no one — not even the admin — can view your actual password. If you forget it, you can reset it.

Tips & Best Practices

  • Clock in every day. If you forget, your services won't count toward your fair-share turn order.
  • Keep customer info up to date. A correct phone number means you can reach them about appointment changes.
  • Use the Waiting List, not the Calendar, for walk-ins. The Calendar is for scheduled bookings.
  • Double-check tip amounts before you click Complete Payment. Changes after payment are harder to fix.
  • Log out on shared computers. It protects your account and the customer's data.
  • Use clear coupon codes. "WEEKEND10" is easier to remember than random letters.
  • Review the Payout Report weekly. Catching any mistakes early is easier than fixing them months later.

Support & Contact

Need help?

  • Ask your admin / manager first. They can reset passwords, fix account issues, and answer most questions.
  • Check the FAQs above. The most common questions are answered there.
  • Report a bug. If something isn't working right, let your admin know with:
    • What you were trying to do
    • What happened instead (a screenshot helps!)
    • The date and time it happened
Reminder: The more detail you give when reporting a problem, the faster it can be fixed.
